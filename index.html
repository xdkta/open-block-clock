    OPEN BLOCK CLOCKPROJECT

    
          
    
          
    
            
    
          
    
        
    
        
    Open source software designed to show
          the Bitcoin block height and more.
    
        
    
          
    Alongside the height it tracks the next halving,
            network fees, the mempool and the price across four screens.
    
          
    Data comes from mempool.space by default, or
            from your own node so nothing leaves your network.
